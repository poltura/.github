### Cleaner, faster and cheaper to run, with AI throughout.

Poltura is an e-commerce platform built for European merchants. Themes, checkout, a customer inbox and an assistant for the day-to-day work come with the shop. Build your store free and pay when you open.

- **Design by describing it.** Drag and drop sections, or tell the AI store designer what you want. Every change waits in a draft you check on desktop, tablet and mobile before it goes live.
- **An assistant that does the work.** Klea updates products, handles orders, writes descriptions and restyles your store, and asks for your approval before money moves or anything goes live.
- **Selling is built in.** Nine payment providers including Stripe, PayPal, Barion and SimplePay, couriers with labels and parcel lockers, and invoices issued through Billingo or Számlázz.hu.
- **One inbox for every customer.** Website chat and email land in one place, with AI drafting the replies.
- **Open to developers.** Theme code in the browser or on your own machine, and an app SDK and CLI for the app platform, which opens to outside developers soon.

<sub><b>Built in the EU.</b> Poltura is made in Hungary. Consent, data requests, VAT and invoicing work the way EU and Hungarian rules expect, from the first day you sell.</sub>
